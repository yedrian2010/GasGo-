GASGO HARDWARE

GasGo Link

● DEMO

GasGo Link is the proposed Bluetooth OBD-II device that can provide additional compatible vehicle data to GasGo.

📡
Bluetooth connection Proposed automatic vehicle data sync
⛽
Range assistance Adds available vehicle data to GasGo estimates
📈
Driving insights Helps build consumption and efficiency history
GasGo Link is designed around OBD-II compatibility. Most U.S.-market light-duty vehicles from model year 1996 or newer use OBD-II, but available data and features vary by vehicle.
⛽ Smart fill-up tracking

When you add a gasoline fill-up to your Fuel Log, GasGo uses the saved tank capacity to update your estimated fuel level and remaining range.

Fuel level and range values in this prototype are user-entered estimates unless compatible vehicle data is available in a future GasGo Link implementation.
