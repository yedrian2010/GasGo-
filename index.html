Compatibility Vehicle dependent
GasGo Link is designed around OBD-II connectivity. Most U.S.-market light-duty vehicles from model year 1996 or newer use OBD-II, but available data and GasGo features can vary by vehicle.
How range works
Estimated, never guaranteed.

GasGo estimates remaining range using the vehicle information available to the app. Compatible vehicles could provide additional information through GasGo Link.

Driving style, traffic, terrain, weather and vehicle condition can change actual range.
